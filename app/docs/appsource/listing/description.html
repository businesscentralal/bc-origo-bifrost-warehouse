Run warehouse work in Business Central from other systems and AI assistants.

Bifrost Warehouse lets a scanner app, a warehouse system, an integration or an AI assistant carry out warehouse tasks in Business Central: receive goods, put them away, pick them and ship them. Every task runs through Business Central's own logic, exactly as if a warehouse worker did it. It is an add-on to Bifrost Foundation.

Who it is for

Companies that run handheld scanners, warehouse or e-commerce systems next to Business Central, and teams that want an assistant to help with day-to-day warehouse work.

What it does

  • Bins and activities: see what is in each bin, and list the open picks, put-aways and other warehouse activities with their lines.
  • Receiving: create warehouse receipts from released purchase orders and other inbound documents, preview the posting and post the receipt.
  • Put-away: create the put-away for a posted receipt and register it when the goods are on the shelf.
  • Picking: create the pick for a warehouse shipment and register it when the goods are picked.
  • Shipping: create warehouse shipments from released sales orders and other outbound documents, preview the posting and post the shipment, with or without invoicing.

Control and safety

  • Posting and registering need their own permission set, so a system can prepare warehouse documents without being able to post them.
  • Invoicing a shipment needs a second permission set.
  • Connected systems cannot write warehouse document records directly through Bifrost; they go through these tasks and Business Central's checks.
  • Every request is checked before anything changes, and errors come back with a clear reason.

Requirements and pricing

  • Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central 28.3 or later, Essentials or Premium.
  • Bifrost Foundation, available separately on AppSource.

Bifrost Warehouse does not replace Business Central or its extensions. It makes their data and business logic available to the people, routines and AI platforms your organisation already uses.
